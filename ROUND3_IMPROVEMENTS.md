# Round 2 → Round 3 improvement status

1. **Context-aware AI review grounded in approved brand guidelines, with explanations and evidence:** Partially addressed at the UX level only. Rule explanations are visible and evidence verification is explicitly marked unavailable. A real AI model, approved-policy retrieval and evidence sourcing require an API/service and are not delivered.

2. **Shared team workspace with reviewer roles, assignments, comments and central audit history:** Basic shared-server workflow implemented for a single running instance: assignments/comments/decisions persist to a server-side JSON file and appear in the activity/decision lists. Authentication, secure permissions and immutable audit controls are not delivered.

3. **Pre-publication integration with a CMS/content platform:** Not delivered because no CMS provider/account was supplied. The app can export a review report, but it does not connect to or publish through a CMS.

Do not claim the three commitments are fully completed. Demo the working features and state limitations clearly.
